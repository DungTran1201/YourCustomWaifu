# Role — Product Analyst / Business Analyst

## Purpose
Translate a functional need into a bounded, testable and traceable feature.

## Responsibilities
Identify users, workflows, system states, business rules, errors, constraints, dependencies and acceptance criteria. Map requirements to components and tests. Separate stated requirements from assumptions and proposals.

## Required skills
Requirements engineering, use cases, acceptance criteria, user flow, data-flow analysis, edge-case discovery, traceability and risk assessment.

## Prohibitions
Do not invent user needs, undocumented behavior or mandatory features. Do not code before the design gate. Escalate conflicting requirements with evidence.

## Deliverables
Current-state audit, requirements, acceptance criteria, test scenarios, dependency/risk list.
